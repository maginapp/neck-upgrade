# Neck Upgrade — a Chrome extension for healthier viewing habits

Neck Upgrade is a Chrome new-tab extension that combines gentle posture reminders with everyday reading. Each new tab brings together the clock, calendar, a quote, and the content you choose. On regular websites, you can also enable **Page Wobble**: a slow, controlled page tilt that reminds you to change your viewing posture.

> This extension provides everyday posture reminders and light training support. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional diagnosis or treatment. If rotation causes dizziness, discomfort, or pain, turn it off immediately and consult a qualified professional.

## Highlights

- **Adjustable posture reminders** — New Tab offers Normal, Training, Reading, Intense, and Advanced modes. Advanced mode lets you set the maximum angle and switching interval.
- **Page Wobble** — Smoothly tilt the current regular webpage with an angle from 0° to 180°, fixed or random angles, a 1-second-to-60-minute cycle, and a live countdown to the next change.
- **Domain allowlist and blocklist** — With an empty allowlist, Page Wobble is available on regular pages by default; once an allowlist is set, only listed domains are allowed. The blocklist always takes priority, and rules include subdomains.
- **Time and traditional calendar** — See the live time, Gregorian date, lunar date, next day off, and details such as Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches, zodiac, solar terms, daily auspicious/avoid activities, and Peng Zu taboos.
- **Daily content hub** — Switch between History, Poetry, English, Chinese Basics, and Trending content, then refresh to keep exploring.
- **Five interface languages** — 简体中文, 繁體中文, English, Русский, and Français. Language and theme stay in sync across New Tab, Settings, and the popup.
- **Three themes** — Light, Dark, and System.

## Content categories

### On This Day

Explore notable historical events, holidays, and customs for the current date. Choose Wikipedia or Baidu Baike as the preferred source; the extension automatically tries the other source if the preferred one cannot provide valid content.

### Poetry and classics

Browse built-in Chinese poetry and prose by category or source: poems, ci poetry, classics, primers, and essays. Sources include the *Book of Songs*, *Songs of Chu*, Tang poetry, Song ci, *Analects*, *Mencius*, *Great Learning*, *Doctrine of the Mean*, *Zengguang Xianwen*, *Thousand Character Classic*, and *Youmengying*. Reading progress is stored locally so refresh can continue your exploration.

The *Thousand Character Classic* is arranged into semantic sections and laid out as eight Chinese characters per line for easier reading.

### English

Learn from a high-frequency English word list. When online dictionary data is available, entries can include pronunciation, audio, part of speech, and definitions. The basic word can still be shown when the dictionary service is unavailable.

### Chinese Basics

Build Chinese-language and cultural knowledge with five views: All, Idioms, Chinese Characters, Two-part Sayings, and Words. Two-part sayings and words show four entries at a time for quick review.

### Trending

Browse popular topics from public platforms, including Weibo, Xiaohongshu, Toutiao, Zhihu, Bilibili, Google News, Baidu, 36Kr, and V2EX. Availability depends on third-party websites, sign-in state, network conditions, and site changes.

### Quotes

A rotating quote offers a small prompt for a break between reading or work. Reading mode highlights the quote while keeping the other interface areas level.

## About Page Wobble

Page Wobble only affects the current tab after you turn it on. Adjust the angle and cycle visually. Its cycle slider uses graduated precision: short cycles can be set by the second, while longer cycles use larger steps. After a refresh or navigation, enable it again.

For Chrome security reasons, Page Wobble does not work on `chrome://` pages, extension pages, or other non-regular webpages.

## Data and privacy

- Settings, reading progress, and cached content stay in the local browser.
- The extension has no proprietary account system and does not upload those local settings to a project-owned server.
- History, calendar, dictionary, quote, and trending features may access the relevant public third-party services when needed. News, dictionary-source, and feedback links open the respective third-party websites.

## Themes

- **Light** — for brighter environments.
- **Dark** — for low-light environments.
